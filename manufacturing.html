Business Technology That Supports the Operation

Manufacturing technology problems do not always stay in the office. A network outage, aging workstation, failed backup, account problem or vendor-access issue can affect the people and systems the operation depends on.

Where Rennie IT Can Help

Support can include business Wi-Fi and switching, firewalls, Windows PCs and servers, Microsoft 365, user administration, endpoint protection, patching, backup and recovery, equipment upgrades, new locations and vendor coordination.

Plant and Office Connectivity

Rennie IT can help troubleshoot and improve the business network connecting offices, work areas, warehouses and supported equipment. Specialized industrial control, PLC and automation engineering are outside this service; the focus is the IT infrastructure around the operation.

Security and Recovery

Review MFA, endpoint protection, patching, backups, restore testing, privileged access and third-party remote access so an everyday IT problem is less likely to become a prolonged business interruption.
