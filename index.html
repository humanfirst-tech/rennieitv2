The Gap We Fill

IT operates the environment. Who keeps the risk work moving?

In many organizations, IT risk, control reviews, audit follow-up and compliance activities become an additional responsibility for people already focused on uptime, users, projects and security operations.

Rennie GRC works alongside the people you already have. We can coordinate with management, internal IT, your MSP, vendors and auditors while maintaining focus on risk throughout the year.
