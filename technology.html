Remediation & Advisory

Move from identifying risk to reducing it.

Advisory, coordination and validation support that helps turn IT risk, audit and compliance findings into measurable remediation.

Risk should lead to action.

Rennie GRC helps translate findings into clear remediation requirements, priorities, ownership and evidence of closure. Implementation can remain with your internal IT team, MSP or technology vendors, preserving a clear focus on risk oversight and validation.

How we help

Define

Translate findings into practical remediation requirements, priorities, owners and target dates.

Coordinate

Work with internal IT, MSPs and technology vendors to keep remediation moving and evidence organized.

Validate

Review evidence, test where appropriate and confirm that corrective actions address the identified risk before closure.

Designed to complement your existing IT resources.

Rennie GRC does not provide ongoing help desk, endpoint management, monitoring or other traditional MSP services. We can define remediation requirements, help evaluate responses and validate closure while your internal team, MSP or technology vendors perform implementation.
