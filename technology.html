Technology & Remediation

Move from finding the risk to fixing it.

Selected technical implementation and remediation support for issues identified through risk, audit and compliance work.

Risk should lead to action.

Some organizations already have the technical resources to remediate findings. Others need help translating a recommendation into a workable solution. Rennie IT can support selected remediation directly or coordinate with your existing IT provider.

Examples

Identity & Access

MFA, account lifecycle, privileged access, permissions and Microsoft 365/Entra security improvements.

Endpoint Security

Endpoint management, patching, EDR/MDR and hardening improvements tied to identified risk.

Backup & Recovery

Backup configuration review, restore testing, recovery procedures and documentation.

Designed to complement—not automatically replace—your IT provider.

The goal is not to turn every GRC relationship into an MSP contract. Where independence or existing responsibilities make it more appropriate, Rennie IT can define the requirement, help evaluate the response and validate remediation while your internal team or MSP performs the implementation.
