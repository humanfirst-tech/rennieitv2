HIPAA Security Risk Assessment

Understand where electronic patient information may be exposed.

Rennie IT provides a structured security risk assessment for independent medical and dental practices, translating technology findings into documented risks, existing safeguards and practical remediation priorities.

Schedule a Consultation

What the Assessment Looks At

The assessment considers where electronic protected health information is created, received, maintained or transmitted; relevant threats and vulnerabilities; existing safeguards; and the likelihood and potential impact of identified risks.

Areas Commonly Reviewed

Depending on the practice environment, review areas may include user access, MFA, workstations, patching, backups and recovery, Microsoft 365, vendor access, remote access, network safeguards and the systems or locations where ePHI exists.

What You Receive

Deliverables include scope and ePHI inventory information, documented risks and existing safeguards, a prioritized risk register and a practical remediation plan.

Important Scope Note

A security risk assessment documents and evaluates risk; it is not a legal opinion or a guarantee of HIPAA compliance. The real assessment must reflect the practice's actual environment and safeguards.
