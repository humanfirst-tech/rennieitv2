Risk & Assurance

Know where your technology risks are before they become business problems.

Rennie IT independently reviews important technology risks and controls and turns the findings into clear priorities. The assessment can complement an existing IT provider rather than replace one.

What We Review

The scope can include identity and access, MFA, endpoint management, patching, backup and recovery, vendor access, remote access, network resilience, administrative privileges and technology recovery practices.

Designed for Established Small Businesses

The assessment is intended for organizations that depend on technology but may not have a dedicated internal security, risk or audit function.

Independent Second Opinion

Already have an MSP or IT provider? That is okay. Rennie IT can assess the controls around the environment and identify questions or gaps for management and the existing provider to address.

From Findings to Priorities

Instead of delivering a list of technical observations with no context, findings can be organized through RiskView as safeguards, indicators, business risks and recommended actions.
